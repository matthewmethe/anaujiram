Diving deeper

Efficiency punishes a deep bench: with fodder behind your starters the right lineup is obvious, but with good players sitting there every week is a coin flip. So each week is also compared with autopilot, the lineup you'd get by starting whoever had the best season-to-date average (last season's helps fill in the first few weeks; players who scored 0 are treated as byes or inactive).

  • Hidden pts: points the best lineup found that autopilot missed. Bigger means the bench made the calls harder.
  • vs autopilot: points gained or lost by not just starting the usual guys.
  • Decision score: the share of those hidden points the manager actually captured. 0% is autopilot; below zero means the tinkering cost points.
